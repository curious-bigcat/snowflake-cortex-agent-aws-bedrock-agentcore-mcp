# ShopSmart International Shipping Guide

**Effective Date**: January 1, 2024
**Policy ID**: SG-INT-2024

## International Shipping Overview

ShopSmart ships to over 50 countries worldwide. International orders are subject to customs duties, taxes, and import regulations that vary by destination country.

## Shipping Options

| Shipping Method | Delivery Time | Cost |
|----------------|---------------|------|
| International Standard | 10-20 business days | $14.99 - $29.99 |
| International Express | 5-8 business days | $34.99 - $49.99 |
| International Priority | 3-5 business days | $59.99 - $79.99 |

Shipping costs vary based on destination country, package weight, and dimensions. The exact shipping cost is calculated at checkout.

## Supported Countries

### Full Service (All shipping methods available)
Canada, United Kingdom, Australia, Germany, France, Japan, South Korea, Singapore, India, Mexico, Brazil, Netherlands, Sweden, Italy, Spain, New Zealand, Ireland.

### Standard Shipping Only
Most other countries in Europe, Asia-Pacific, South America, and the Middle East. Check availability at checkout.

### Restricted Countries
ShopSmart does not currently ship to: North Korea, Iran, Syria, Cuba, Sudan, or any country subject to comprehensive US trade sanctions.

## Customs, Duties, and Taxes

- **ShopSmart does not collect customs duties or import taxes.** These are the responsibility of the buyer.
- Customs duties and taxes are determined by the destination country's customs authority and are collected upon delivery.
- Typical duty rates range from **5% to 25%** depending on the product type and country.
- Some countries have a de minimis threshold below which no duties are charged (e.g., Australia: AUD $1,000; Canada: CAD $20).

## Documentation

All international shipments include:
- Commercial invoice (auto-generated)
- Customs declaration form
- Product descriptions, values, and harmonized tariff codes

## Restricted Items for International Shipping

The following items cannot be shipped internationally:
- Lithium batteries exceeding 100Wh (standalone, not installed in a device)
- Certain electronics subject to export controls (ITAR/EAR)
- Hazardous materials
- Items prohibited by destination country regulations

**Note**: Laptops, smartphones, and other devices with built-in lithium batteries CAN be shipped internationally as they are below the 100Wh threshold.

## Returns for International Orders

- International returns follow the same return window as domestic orders.
- **Return shipping cost is the buyer's responsibility** for international orders.
- Customs duties paid on the original shipment are **not refundable** by ShopSmart (contact your customs authority for duty refund procedures).
- Refunds are processed to the original payment method in the original currency.

## Delivery Issues

For international delivery issues:
1. Allow an extra **5 business days** beyond the estimated delivery window before contacting support (customs clearance may cause delays).
2. If your package is held by customs, you may need to provide additional documentation to your local customs office.
3. Contact ShopSmart support if the package has not arrived within **30 days** of shipment.

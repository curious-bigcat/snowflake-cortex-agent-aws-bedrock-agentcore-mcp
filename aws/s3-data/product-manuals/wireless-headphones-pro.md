## Pairing Instructions

### First-Time Bluetooth Pairing

1. Press and hold the **Power button** on the right ear cup for 3 seconds. The LED will flash blue.
2. On your device, go to **Settings > Bluetooth** and look for "WHP-2024" in the available devices list.
3. Tap to connect. You will hear a voice prompt: "Connected."
4. The LED will turn solid blue when paired.

### Multipoint Connection

The Wireless Headphones Pro supports connection to two devices simultaneously.

1. Pair with the first device as described above.
2. Turn off Bluetooth on the first device.
3. Put headphones in pairing mode again (hold Power for 3 seconds).
4. Pair with the second device.
5. Turn Bluetooth back on for the first device. Both devices are now connected.

## Controls

| Button | Short Press | Long Press (3s) |
|--------|-------------|-----------------|
| Power | Play/Pause | Power On/Off |
| ANC | Toggle ANC modes | Transparency mode |
| Volume + | Volume Up | Next Track |
| Volume - | Volume Down | Previous Track |

## Active Noise Cancellation

Three ANC modes are available:
- **High ANC**: Best for flights and noisy environments. Reduces ambient noise by up to 35dB.
- **Low ANC**: Moderate noise reduction for office environments.
- **Transparency**: Lets ambient sound through so you can hear announcements or conversations.

## Charging

- Connect the USB-C cable to the charging port on the right ear cup.
- Full charge time: approximately 2 hours.
- Battery life: up to 40 hours with ANC off, 30 hours with ANC on.
- Quick charge: 10 minutes of charging provides 3 hours of playback.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| No sound | Ensure headphones are paired and volume is up on both headphone and device. |
| Poor audio quality | Move closer to paired device. Check for Bluetooth interference. |
| ANC not working | Ensure ANC is enabled. Check that ear cups form a proper seal around your ears. |
| Won't charge | Try a different USB-C cable. Clean the charging port. |
| Bluetooth drops | Reset headphones: hold Power + ANC buttons for 10 seconds. |

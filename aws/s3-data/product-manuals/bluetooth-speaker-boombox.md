## Pairing Instructions

1. Press the **Power button** on the top of the speaker. The LED ring will pulse blue.
2. The speaker enters pairing mode automatically when first powered on.
3. On your device, go to **Settings > Bluetooth** and select "BSB-2024".
4. You will hear a tone confirming the connection.

### PartyLink (Multi-Speaker Mode)

Connect up to 4 BoomBox speakers for synchronized playback:

1. Power on all speakers.
2. On the primary speaker, double-press the **PartyLink button** (speaker icon).
3. On each secondary speaker, press the **PartyLink button** once.
4. All speakers will sync within 5 seconds.

## Controls

- **Power**: Press to turn on/off.
- **Volume +/-**: Adjust volume (15 levels).
- **Play/Pause**: Single press to play/pause. Double press to skip track.
- **PartyLink**: Connect multiple speakers.
- **Mic button**: Activate voice assistant (Siri/Google Assistant).

## Sound Modes

Three EQ presets selectable by pressing Volume+ and Volume- simultaneously:
- **Standard**: Balanced sound for all genres.
- **Bass Boost**: Enhanced low frequencies for EDM, hip-hop, and pop.
- **Outdoor**: Louder output optimized for open-air environments.

## Water Resistance

The BoomBox is rated **IPX7**, meaning it can be submerged in up to 1 meter of fresh water for 30 minutes. It is safe for:
- Pool parties, beach use, rain, and showers.

**Do NOT**:
- Submerge in salt water (rinse with fresh water if exposed).
- Charge while wet. Ensure the USB-C port cover is fully closed before water exposure.

## Charging

- Connect the USB-C cable to the port under the rubber flap on the back.
- Full charge time: approximately 3.5 hours.
- Battery life: 20 hours at 50% volume.
- Battery indicator: LED ring shows green (>50%), yellow (20-50%), red (<20%).

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| No sound | Ensure Bluetooth is connected. Check volume on both speaker and device. |
| Distorted audio | Lower the volume. Switch to Standard sound mode. |
| Won't pair | Reset speaker: hold Power + Volume- for 10 seconds. |
| Short battery life | Reduce volume. Disable PartyLink if not needed. Ensure latest firmware. |
| Water damage suspected | Dry thoroughly. Do not charge for at least 4 hours. Contact support if issues persist. |

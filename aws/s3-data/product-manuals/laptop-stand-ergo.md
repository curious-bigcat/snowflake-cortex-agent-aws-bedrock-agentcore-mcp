## Assembly Instructions

The Laptop Stand Ergo comes pre-assembled. No tools are required for basic use.

### Adjusting the Height

1. Hold the base of the stand with one hand.
2. With the other hand, lift the top platform to the desired angle.
3. The friction hinge holds the position automatically.
4. Supported angles: 15 to 45 degrees from horizontal.

### Adjusting Hinge Tension

If the hinge becomes too loose or too tight over time:

1. Locate the hinge screws on both sides of the stand.
2. Use the included Allen wrench.
3. Turn clockwise to tighten, counter-clockwise to loosen.
4. Test with your laptop in place to ensure it holds securely.

## Ergonomic Guidelines

For optimal ergonomic positioning:
- Set the stand so the **top of your laptop screen is at eye level**.
- Use an **external keyboard and mouse** when using the stand to avoid neck strain.
- Position the stand approximately **20-26 inches** (50-65 cm) from your eyes.
- Take breaks every 30-45 minutes to stand and stretch.

## Care and Maintenance

- Wipe with a damp cloth. Do not use abrasive cleaners.
- The silicone pads can be replaced if they wear out (extras included in box).
- Do not exceed the maximum load of 10kg.
- Store folded flat when not in use.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| Laptop slides off | Check that silicone pads are in place. Clean pads with damp cloth. |
| Hinge won't hold position | Tighten hinge screws with Allen wrench. |
| Wobbling on desk | Ensure all 4 rubber feet on the base are intact. Place on flat surface. |
| Scratches on laptop | Replace worn silicone pads. Do not place laptop directly on bare metal. |

# ShopSmart Domestic Shipping Guide

**Effective Date**: January 1, 2024
**Policy ID**: SG-DOM-2024

## Shipping Options

ShopSmart offers multiple shipping options for deliveries within the continental United States, Alaska, and Hawaii.

| Shipping Method | Delivery Time | Cost | Free Threshold |
|----------------|---------------|------|----------------|
| Standard Shipping | 5-7 business days | $5.99 | Free on orders over $35 |
| Expedited Shipping | 2-3 business days | $12.99 | Free on orders over $75 |
| Next-Day Shipping | 1 business day | $24.99 | Free for Premium members |
| Same-Day Delivery | Same day (order by 2 PM local time) | $14.99 | Select metro areas only |

**ShopSmart Premium members** receive free Standard and Expedited shipping on all orders, regardless of order value.

## Same-Day Delivery Availability

Same-day delivery is currently available in the following metro areas:
- New York City, Los Angeles, Chicago, Houston, Phoenix, San Francisco, Seattle, Denver, Miami, Atlanta, Boston, Dallas, Washington DC.

Eligibility: Orders placed by 2:00 PM local time for in-stock items under 30 lbs.

## Order Processing

- Orders placed **before 2:00 PM ET** on business days are processed the same day.
- Orders placed **after 2:00 PM ET** or on weekends/holidays are processed the next business day.
- Processing time is typically **1 business day** and is not included in the delivery time estimates above.

## Tracking Your Order

1. You will receive a shipping confirmation email with a tracking number once your order ships.
2. Track your package at www.shopsmart.com/track or via the ShopSmart app.
3. Real-time delivery notifications are available through the app.

## Delivery Instructions

When placing your order, you can add delivery instructions such as:
- Leave at front door, back porch, or with a neighbor.
- Require signature (recommended for orders over $200).
- Hold at carrier facility for pickup.

## Large and Heavy Items

Items over **50 lbs** or items with dimensions exceeding **108 inches** in combined length and girth:
- Standard and Expedited shipping only. Next-Day and Same-Day not available.
- Additional freight charges of **$29.99 - $49.99** may apply depending on item size.
- White-glove delivery (room of choice, assembly) available for furniture: **$79.99**.

## Missing, Lost, or Stolen Packages

If your package is lost or stolen:
1. Check tracking for delivery confirmation and any photos.
2. Contact ShopSmart support within **7 days** of the expected delivery date.
3. We will investigate with the carrier and either reship the order or issue a full refund within 3-5 business days.

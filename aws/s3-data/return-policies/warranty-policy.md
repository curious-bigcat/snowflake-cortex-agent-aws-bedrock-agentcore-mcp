# ShopSmart Warranty Policy

**Effective Date**: January 1, 2024
**Policy ID**: WP-2024

## Standard Warranty Coverage

All products sold by ShopSmart include a manufacturer's warranty. The warranty period varies by product category:

| Category | Warranty Period |
|----------|---------------|
| Laptops and Tablets | 2 years |
| Headphones and Audio | 1 year |
| Smartwatches and Wearables | 1 year |
| Home Appliances | 2 years |
| Accessories (cables, stands, cases) | 6 months |
| Furniture | 5 years (structural defects only) |

## What's Covered

The warranty covers defects in **materials and workmanship** under normal use, including:

- Hardware failures not caused by user error or damage (e.g., dead pixels, battery failure, broken buttons).
- Software defects present at the time of purchase that prevent normal operation.
- Components that stop functioning within the warranty period under normal use.

## What's Not Covered

The warranty does **NOT** cover:

1. **Accidental damage**: Drops, spills, impacts, or physical breakage.
2. **Water damage**: Beyond the product's rated water resistance level (e.g., submerging a non-waterproof device).
3. **Unauthorized modifications**: Repairs or modifications performed by non-authorized service providers.
4. **Consumable parts**: Batteries are covered for 6 months only. Expected battery degradation over time is not a defect.
5. **Cosmetic damage**: Scratches, dents, or fading that do not affect functionality.
6. **Damage from misuse**: Using the product in ways not described in the user manual.

## How to File a Warranty Claim

1. Contact ShopSmart Support at 1-800-SHOP-SMART or warranty@shopsmart.com.
2. Provide your **order number**, **product model**, and a **description of the issue**.
3. Include **photos or video** demonstrating the defect.
4. Our warranty team will review and respond within **2 business days**.

If the claim is approved:
- **Repair**: We will repair the product at no cost and return it within 7-10 business days.
- **Replacement**: If repair is not feasible, we will send a replacement of the same model. If the model is discontinued, an equivalent or upgraded model will be provided.
- **Refund**: If neither repair nor replacement is available, a full refund will be issued.

## Warranty Transfer

Warranties are tied to the original purchaser and are **non-transferable**. If you gift a product, the recipient must contact support with the original order details to file a claim.

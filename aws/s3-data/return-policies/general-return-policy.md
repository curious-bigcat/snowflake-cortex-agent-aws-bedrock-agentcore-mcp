# ShopSmart General Return and Refund Policy

**Effective Date**: January 1, 2024
**Policy ID**: RP-GEN-2024

## Overview

ShopSmart is committed to customer satisfaction. If you are not completely satisfied with your purchase, you may return most items within **14 calendar days** of delivery for a full refund or exchange. Certain product categories have extended return windows.

## Return Windows by Category

| Category | Return Window | Notes |
|----------|--------------|-------|
| Electronics | 30 days | See Electronics Return Policy (RP-ELEC-2024) |
| Clothing & Apparel | 14 days | Must be unworn with tags attached |
| Home & Kitchen | 14 days | Must be unused and in original packaging |
| Books & Media | 14 days | Physical items only; digital not returnable |
| Furniture | 7 days | Must be unassembled; assembled items not returnable |
| Food & Beverages | Not returnable | Health and safety regulations apply |
| ShopSmart Premium Members | +30 days | Added to standard window for all categories |

## Refund Methods

1. **Original Payment Method**: Default refund method. Processing time is 5-7 business days after the return is inspected.
2. **ShopSmart Store Credit**: Issued within 24 hours. Store credit never expires.
3. **Gift Card Purchases**: Refunded as store credit only.

## Return Shipping

- **Free returns**: All returns shipped via our prepaid labels are free.
- **Self-shipped returns**: If you choose your own carrier, shipping costs are your responsibility.
- **In-store returns**: Bring the item and proof of purchase to any ShopSmart retail location for immediate processing.

## Items That Cannot Be Returned

- Perishable goods (food, flowers, plants)
- Personal care items (cosmetics, hygiene products) once opened
- Gift cards and downloadable software
- Items marked "Final Sale" or "Non-Returnable"
- Hazardous materials (batteries sold separately, cleaning chemicals)
- Items that have been altered, modified, or repaired by third parties

## Damaged or Incorrect Items

If you receive a damaged or incorrect item:
1. Report the issue within **48 hours** of delivery.
2. Provide photos of the damage or incorrect item.
3. We will arrange a free pickup and send the correct item or issue a full refund.
4. You do not need to return damaged items under $25 in value.

## Late Returns

Returns received after the return window has closed may be:
- Accepted with a **25% restocking fee** (at our discretion).
- Declined entirely if the product is used, damaged, or missing components.

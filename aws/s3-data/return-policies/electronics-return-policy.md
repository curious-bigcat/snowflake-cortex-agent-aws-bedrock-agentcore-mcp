# ShopSmart Electronics Return Policy

**Effective Date**: January 1, 2024
**Policy ID**: RP-ELEC-2024

## Overview

ShopSmart offers a **30-day return window** for all electronics products purchased through our website or retail stores. This policy applies to laptops, headphones, speakers, smartwatches, tablets, and all accessories in the Electronics category.

## Return Window

- **Standard returns**: 30 calendar days from the date of delivery.
- **Holiday purchases** (November 15 - December 31): Extended return window until January 31 of the following year.
- **ShopSmart Premium members**: 60-day return window on all electronics.

## Conditions for Return

To be eligible for a return, the following conditions must be met:

1. **Original packaging**: The product must be returned in its original packaging with all included accessories, manuals, and cables.
2. **Condition**: The product must be in good working condition. Minor cosmetic wear from normal evaluation is acceptable.
3. **Proof of purchase**: A valid receipt, order confirmation email, or order number is required.
4. **No physical damage**: Products with physical damage (cracks, water damage beyond rated resistance, bent connectors) are not eligible for return.

## Restocking Fees

| Product Condition | Restocking Fee |
|-------------------|---------------|
| Unopened, sealed in original packaging | No fee |
| Opened, in like-new condition | No fee |
| Opened, with minor wear | 10% restocking fee |
| Missing accessories or packaging | 15% restocking fee |

## Refund Process

- **Original payment method**: Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after the return is received and inspected.
- **Store credit**: Available as an alternative, processed within 24 hours of return receipt.
- **ShopSmart Gift Cards**: Purchases made with gift cards are refunded as store credit only.

## How to Initiate a Return

1. Log in to your ShopSmart account at www.shopsmart.com.
2. Navigate to **Orders > Order History**.
3. Select the order containing the item you wish to return.
4. Click **Return Item** and select the reason for return.
5. Print the prepaid return shipping label.
6. Pack the item securely and drop it off at any authorized shipping location.

Alternatively, you can call 1-800-SHOP-SMART (1-800-746-7762) to initiate a return by phone.

## Non-Returnable Items

The following electronics items cannot be returned:
- **Personalized or custom-engraved items** (e.g., engraved AirPods).
- **Software licenses and digital downloads** once activated.
- **Items marked as Final Sale** on the product page.
- **Items purchased more than 30 days ago** (or 60 days for Premium members) unless covered by warranty.

## Exchanges

If you wish to exchange a product for a different color or model:
1. Initiate a return for the original item.
2. Place a new order for the desired item.
3. Refund for the returned item will be processed as described above.

Direct exchanges are available in-store only.

## Defective Products

If you receive a defective product:
- Contact support within 48 hours of delivery.
- We will arrange a free return and send a replacement immediately.
- No restocking fee applies to defective returns.
- If the same model is out of stock, a full refund will be issued.

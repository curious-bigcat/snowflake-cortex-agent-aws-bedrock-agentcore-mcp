# SmartWatch X1 - User Manual

## Product Overview

The **SmartWatch X1** by ShopSmart Tech is a feature-rich smartwatch with health monitoring, GPS, and 7-day battery life. Compatible with iOS 15+ and Android 10+. Model number: SWX1-2024.

- **Price**: $249.99
- **Display**: 1.4" AMOLED, 454x454 resolution
- **Water Resistance**: 5ATM (swimproof)
- **Warranty**: 1-year limited warranty

## Initial Setup

### Charging Before First Use

1. Place the SmartWatch X1 on the magnetic charging dock.
2. Ensure the charging pins align with the contacts on the back of the watch.
3. The battery icon will appear on screen. Charge fully before first use (approximately 2 hours).

### App Installation and Pairing

1. Download the **ShopSmart Health** app from the App Store or Google Play.
2. Create an account or sign in.
3. In the app, tap **Add Device > SmartWatch X1**.
4. Hold the watch near your phone. The app will detect and pair automatically.
5. Follow the on-screen prompts to complete setup (notifications, health permissions).

## Features

### Health Monitoring

- **Heart Rate**: Continuous optical heart rate monitoring. Alerts if resting heart rate exceeds 100 BPM or drops below 40 BPM.
- **Blood Oxygen (SpO2)**: On-demand and nightly SpO2 measurements. Normal range: 95-100%.
- **Sleep Tracking**: Tracks light, deep, and REM sleep stages. Provides sleep score each morning.
- **Stress Monitoring**: Measures stress level on a scale of 1-100 using heart rate variability (HRV).
- **Step Counter**: Tracks daily steps. Default goal: 10,000 steps.

### GPS and Navigation

- Built-in GPS for outdoor activities (running, cycling, hiking).
- Supports GPS, GLONASS, and Galileo satellite systems.
- Route tracking with distance, pace, and elevation data.

### Notifications

- Mirror phone notifications on your wrist (calls, messages, apps).
- Quick reply to messages with preset responses or voice-to-text.
- Do Not Disturb and Sleep modes available.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| Watch won't turn on | Place on charger for at least 30 minutes. Press and hold side button for 5 seconds. |
| Heart rate not reading | Wear watch snugly 1-2 finger widths above wrist bone. Clean sensor. |
| GPS not locking | Go outdoors to open sky. Wait 30 seconds for GPS lock. |
| Notifications not showing | Check phone Bluetooth is on. Verify notification permissions in ShopSmart Health app. |
| App won't pair | Restart both phone and watch. Ensure Bluetooth is on and phone is within 1 meter. |
